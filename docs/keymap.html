Combos

The four Voyager combos are pinned to layers = <BASE>, and Settings to <EXT>. Without that they ran on whichever layer was active, so on the Mods layer typing 45 fired Tab and 67 fired Backspace. Positions 4 and 5 now carry two combos each, on different layers — they never contend.

Why the shortcuts are macros

Apple's Globe shortcuts are documented for a real Apple keyboard. ZMK cannot hold GLOBE down as a modifier, so each one is emitted as a macro instead — press GLOBE, tap the key, release GLOBE. Same HID traffic as a real chord, one keypress for you.

And there is no separate Fn key to map: Fn and Globe are the same physical key on Apple keyboards, and the real one is AppleVendor Top Case page 0xFF usage 0x03, gated on Apple's own vendor ID. ZMK cannot send it. What it sends is consumer usage 0x029D, which covers keyboard/emoji switching and some Globe chords — test Globe+H on the iPad before trusting it.

There is no Sym layer any more: 28 of its 30 keys were reachable as shift + something on the Mods layer, which is why it never had an activator in the first place. PgUp and PgDn were the exceptions and moved here.

The standard keymap (config/urchin.keymap) is now the same four layers; it just lacks the five Voyager combos, so its only combo is Settings.

Both files pass dtc with all layers at 34 bindings. Behavior on hardware is unverified.
